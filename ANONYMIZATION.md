# Anonymization

This repository is prepared as a public portfolio case study.

The public version removes or generalizes:

- original company and product names
- company-specific email addresses and domains
- internal links and identifiers
- real or realistic personal contact information
- sample agency identity, address, tax information and bank-account data
- organization-specific operational references

The following are intentionally preserved because they are part of the product problem being demonstrated:

- the B2B travel-agency onboarding context
- the three-step flow
- Domestic vs. International branching
- conditional agreement behavior
- field dependencies and validation logic
- final summary and notification concepts

All visible names and sample values in this repository should be treated as fictional demonstration data.
